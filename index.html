How these numbers are worked out

  • Turnover rate = employees who left during 2025 ÷ employees in the 2024-12-31 baseline.
  • Estimated replacement cost adds the three cost estimates in compensation_training for each 2025 leaver. They are the client's estimates, not measured costs.
  • Survey scores are means over the employees who answered the single December 2024 survey; 5 is assumed to be the most favourable.
  • Exit reasons are what leavers reported, not measured causes, and only some leavers were interviewed.
  • Every figure is recomputed in your browser from a fresh read of the live database each time you sign in or press Refresh. Nothing is cached or saved.
